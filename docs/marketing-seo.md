# Landing Page + SEO + Marketing Plan

## Positioning
**Research Copilot** is the AI operating system for researchers from idea to publication.

## Landing Page Structure
1. Hero: problem + value proposition + CTA
2. Social proof / institutions
3. Feature grid by lifecycle stage
4. Demo walkthrough video
5. Pricing tiers
6. FAQ (security, citation accuracy, privacy)
7. CTA footer

## SEO Strategy

### Core Keyword Clusters
- AI research assistant
- PDF summarizer for research papers
- literature review AI tool
- citation generator APA IEEE BibTeX
- reviewer simulator for academic papers

### Programmatic SEO Pages
- `/citation/[style]` for style-specific guides
- `/journal-recommendation/[field]`
- `/conference-deadlines/[discipline]`
- `/research-gap/[topic]`

### Content Engine
- Weekly benchmark posts on model quality in research tasks
- Case studies by field (CS, bio, medicine, social science)
- Research workflow templates and checklists

## Marketing Channels
- Academic communities (Reddit, X, Discord, LinkedIn)
- University ambassadors
- Partnerships with labs and tool ecosystems
- Product Hunt + newsletter launches
