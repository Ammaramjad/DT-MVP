# Investor Pitch Deck (Content)

## 1. Problem
Researchers lose massive time across fragmented tools: reading, ideation, writing, citations, and submission strategy.

## 2. Solution
Research Copilot unifies the full research lifecycle in one AI-native platform.

## 3. Product
- AI PDF Reader + Summarizer
- Gap Finder + Experiment Planner
- Reviewer Simulator + Paper Scoring
- Academic Writer + Submission Recommendations

## 4. Market
Global higher-education and R&D software market is large, recurring, and expanding with AI adoption.

## 5. Business Model
- Starter $20/mo
- Pro $35/mo
- Team $50/user/mo
- Enterprise contracts

## 6. Moat
- Workflow depth across full lifecycle
- Proprietary research interaction data
- Grounded RAG with transparent evidence trails
- Team-level collaboration and institutional workflows

## 7. Go-To-Market
- Student/professor adoption loops
- University lab pilots
- R&D B2B expansion

## 8. Traction Metrics
- WAU researchers
- Papers indexed
- Active projects
- Conversion and expansion

## 9. Financial Potential
High gross-margin SaaS with strong expansion via teams and enterprise.

## 10. Ask
Capital and distribution partnerships to accelerate product scale and institutional adoption.
