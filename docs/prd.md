# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product
Research Copilot  
Tagline: **Your AI Research Assistant from Idea to Publication.**

## Vision
Build the all-in-one operating system for researchers, combining document intelligence, planning, writing, and publication strategy.

## Personas
- PhD/MS students
- Professors/labs
- R&D teams and AI researchers
- University research offices

## Core Jobs-to-be-Done
1. Understand papers quickly.
2. Identify valuable research opportunities.
3. Design sound experiments.
4. Produce high-quality manuscripts.
5. Increase acceptance probability.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- P95 API latency < 600ms (non-generation paths)
- Multi-tenant security with strict authorization
- Horizontal scalability to 100k+ users
- Full observability (logs, traces, metrics)
- GDPR-aligned data controls

## Pricing
- Starter: $20/mo

## Success Metrics
- Weekly active researchers
- Papers ingested/user/week
- AI action completion rate
- Manuscript acceptance-likelihood uplift proxy
